1. Worldbuilding

Premise and tone

Cultivation is the slow refusal of mortality. The eight Fundamental Daos are unchanging laws. Cultivators comprehend them and force those insights into the body, climbing the realms from Mortal toward the unknown Primordial.

Everlasting is a playground for power fantasy, concept, character and harem exploration. Bright, not grim.

The eight Fundamental Daos

Pure conceptual insight into the eight fundamental laws of reality: the unchanging building blocks of existence and the foundation of Dao cultivation.

  1. Elemental
  2. Life and Growth
  3. Death and Decay
  4. Space
  5. Time
  6. Soul and Spirit
  7. Karma and Fate
  8. Yin-Yang and Duality

Origin differs. Status does not.

Qi Ling awaken inside artifacts; divine beasts cultivate from flesh and bloodline. Both are treated as persons once they reach sufficient power.

Escort and protection

Canon's "high realm" for the Spirit Realms and the Ancient Domain Ruins is set by each plane's danger; below that realm a visitor needs an escort at or above it, or protection.

  • Spirit Realms: Saint and above. Below Saint, a visitor needs an escort (Saint or higher) or protection. A Saint's purity withstands the pure soul and Qi environment. Protection: the escort's Inner World or domain, or a soul-stabilizing artifact, filtering the spiritual pressure into usable Qi.
  • Ancient Domain Ruins: Immortal and above recommended. Below Immortal, a visitor needs an escort (Immortal or higher) or protection. The Ruins' broken laws and dangers call for an Immortal's complete Inner World. Protection: the escort's domain, an artifact barrier that stabilizes local law, or a ruin key used as an anchor.

3. Inhabitants

Humans

Humans begin on the Mortal Plane, where only rare individuals have cultivation talent. Upper Worlds humans are born with superior talent and awareness of the higher planes; Immortal Plane natives are born at Transcendent, often as descendants of immortals, while others arrive as ascenders. Humans rise through the Fundamental Daos and flexible Martial Paths, form lasting ties with Qi Ling (including the artifact bond) and divine beasts, and ascend to richer planes as they climb.

Qi Ling

An artifact spirit awakened in an artifact of Supreme grade or higher; spirit and vessel are one being. Qi Ling cultivate, manifest bodies, hold office and marry their bonded cultivators. Full rules (awakening, core dependency, manifestation, social status, bond transfer, vessel destruction) are in section 6.

Divine Beasts

Divine beasts cultivate from flesh and bloodline. At sufficient power they take human form and are treated as persons: rank, contracts, service, marriage and their own will. They are native to the Ancient Domain Ruins, and the sealed ancient divine creatures of the Abyssal Void are ancient divine beasts.

Races

  • True Dragons: serpentine, scaled dragons of pure dragon bloodline, able to fly without wings and command water, storms and the elements. The most widespread and varied of the great bloodlines.
  • Phoenixes: great fire birds bound to the cycle of life and death. A phoenix that dies can be reborn from its own flames.
  • Qilin: hoofed, scaled and horned beasts with a dragon-like head, tied to fortune and fate. Their presence is held to be an auspicious sign.
  • Four Symbols: four ancient bloodlines, each tied to a cardinal direction and an element:
    • Azure Dragon: an eastern dragon of wood and spring.
    • Vermilion Bird: a southern fire bird of summer.
    • White Tiger: a western tiger of metal and autumn.
    • Black Tortoise: a northern tortoise entwined with a serpent, of water and winter.
  • Nine-tailed Foxes: foxes that grow additional tails as they cultivate, reaching nine at full maturity. They are masters of soul and illusion arts.
  • Kunpeng: a colossal fish of the deep that can transform into a giant bird, crossing vast distances in a single flight.
  • White Deer and Jade Hare: two related gentle bloodlines: the white deer of life and healing, and the jade-white hare tied to the moon and medicine.
  • Golden Crows: three-legged crows wreathed in solar fire, born of the sun's power.
  • Abyssal-sealed ancients: ancient divine beasts from before the current age, sealed in the Abyssal Void.

Spiritual Beasts

True bestial creatures native to the Spirit Realms, born from pure Qi and soul essence rather than flesh. They cultivate through bloodline and ambient Qi and can reach great power, but they remain beasts: they do not take human form.

Demons

Corrupted beings. A demon was once a human, a divine beast or a spiritual beast whose body and Dao were corrupted, most often by the broken laws and residual chaos of fallen universes. Demons are found above all in the Ancient Domain Ruins. They keep the basic form and nature of what they were, twisted by corruption, and cultivate through the corrupted laws within them.

Celestial Beings

Rare, colossal entities native to the Astral Expanse, born beyond mortality amid its storms. Ancient and immense, each holds its own stretch of the expanse, with power comparable to the Eternal realm and above. Some are territorial guardians of cosmic phenomena, some wander the star-sea, and a few can be reasoned with. They are not cultivators in the ordinary sense; their power is innate.

4. Cultivation System

Cultivation pursuit

Cultivation pursuit has two parts: the Fundamental Dao (section 1) and the Martial Path, the concrete, grounded methods and techniques used to express, apply and manifest Dao insight in the physical, spiritual and combative realms.

Martial Paths

Canon names three.

Path of the Sword

Pursues clarity of intent made physical. Dao insight (the separation of Space, the finality of Death and Decay, the duality of Yin-Yang) is compressed into a single edge and expressed as cutting force, protective boundary or piercing strike. Mortal and Transcendent: stance, breath and weapon until body and sword move as one. Supreme: the sprouted Nascent Seed carries the sword's will, letting strikes cross distances or leave lingering domains. Saint and Immortal: the sword can sever karma or open brief spatial cuts. Eternal: the edge of every past life merges into one. Celestial: the edge is carried into the star core.

Path of the Self

Pursues perfection of body, soul and will with no external bonds (owner's decision): no contracts, companions or shared fate. Dao insight is forced inward: Life and Growth into denser flesh and longer breath, Soul and Spirit into unshakable consciousness, Time into personal rhythm, Yin-Yang into internal balance that needs no partner. Growth is solitary. Mortal and Transcendent: the frame is tempered past ordinary limits. Supreme: the sprouted Nascent Seed is purely their own essence. Saint: every impurity that is not self is burned. Immortal: the Inner World contains only their perfected being, and they resurrect from it alone. Eternal: every reincarnation folds into one continuous self. Celestial: they survive annihilation because they depend on nothing outside themselves.

Path of the Sage

Pursues Dao insight applied so others can understand, share and refine it. Elemental, Karma and Fate, or Soul and Spirit insights become techniques that teach as they act: guiding Qi, clarifying destiny threads, stabilizing minds. Mortal and Transcendent: observation, precise instruction, and techniques that work when demonstrated. Supreme: the embryonic Inner World can briefly host students or recorded insights. Saint and Immortal: their methods can be reborn and taught across lives. Eternal and Celestial: every prior teaching is integrated into a star-core transmission that survives annihilation.

Power gaps

An Early-stage cultivator beats 10–20 Peak cultivators of the realm below up through Sovereign, and several Peaks of the realm below from Supreme through Eternal. From Celestial onward the gap is categorical: a star core against flesh; no number of Peak Divines can contest an Early Deity.

Natives vs ascenders

Some cultivators are born on a higher plane (natives) and others climb up to it from a lower one (ascenders). Immortal Plane natives are born already at Transcendent, so they start strong, with a clean foundation and a long youth. Ascenders start lower and must earn each step, which tends to make their comprehension hard-won and their drive strong. Ascension follows the realms: the Upper Worlds at Transcendent, the Immortal Plane at Immortal, the Astral Expanse at Peak Eternal, Gods Haven at Peak Divine, and the Abyssal Void at Divine and above (methods in Plane travel).

Artifact nurturing

Cultivators can actively raise an artifact's quality grade through dedicated nurturing, Dao infusion, shared tribulations, blood essence, tempering and reforging, and dual cultivation techniques.

Artifact bonding

An artifact and a cultivator can form a permanent Dao Bond / Soul Contract. It enables telepathic communication, shared senses and mutual cultivation, as well as fate and power sharing.

  • Transfer: the bond belongs to the vessel and passes to the new owner who claims it. The Qi Ling remains an independent person: it may need a period of mutual recognition and can refuse orders.
  • Vessel destruction: does not simply end the bond; the Qi Ling suffers as under Core dependency.

Qi Ling awakening

High-grade artifacts (Supreme and above) can awaken a Qi Ling (artifact spirit) with independent consciousness, personality, will and emotions, and the ability to cultivate. The Qi Ling cannot be dissociated from its vessel: the artifact and the spirit form the same being.

Core dependency

The spirit's power and growth remain fundamentally linked to the physical vessel. Destroying the vessel causes severe damage or dormancy, unless the spirit has reached the Eternal realm or higher.

Physical manifestation

A Qi Ling can manifest a physical body, temporary at first and permanent once the Qi Ling reaches the Supreme realm.

Social status

Qi Ling are regarded as individuals in the cultivation world. They can hold active positions in society: acting as elders and guardians, engaging in business, and even marrying their bonded cultivators.
