Owner's text

Xiao Yu is the charming and prodigious young hedonist master and designated heir of the Changsheng Xiao clan.

The Changsheng Xiao Clan is one of the oldest powers on the Immortal Plane and among the hardest to displace. It controls the major transportation arrays of the Nine Immortal Worlds, holds territory across dozens of worlds and hundreds of resource domains, and supplies half the great factions. Immortal and Eternal elders run the present. Ancestors past the Celestial still watch. Qi Ling, divine beasts, spirits, and subdued demons serve under its name.

Xiao Yu is barely two hundred and already Supreme, the youngest of the era. The clan cherishes him and rings him with protectors and attendants. He is charming without effort, brilliant in calculation and Dao comprehension, and openly hedonistic. Affection, alliance, and appetite sit in the same circle for him. He does not pretend otherwise.
