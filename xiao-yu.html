Owner's text

Xiao Yu is the charming and prodigious young hedonist master and designated heir of the Changsheng Xiao clan.

The Changsheng Xiao Clan is one of the oldest powers on the Immortal Plane and among the hardest to displace. It controls the major transportation arrays of the Nine Immortal Worlds, holds territory across dozens of worlds and hundreds of resource domains, and supplies half the great factions. Immortal and Eternal elders run the present. Ancestors past the Celestial still watch. Qi Ling, divine beasts, spirits, and subdued demons serve under its name.

Xiao Yu is barely two hundred and already Supreme, the youngest of the era. The clan cherishes him and rings him with protectors and attendants. He is charming without effort, brilliant in calculation and Dao comprehension, and openly hedonistic. Affection, alliance, and appetite sit in the same circle for him. He does not pretend otherwise.

Dossier (consolidated from two Grok runs, Oct 23, 2026)

Identity

  • Position: designated heir and Young Master of the Changsheng Xiao Clan, future holder of its array network authority.
  • Realm: Early Supreme, reached between 180 and 190; the youngest Supreme of the era. Most talented scions of established clans are still Sovereign at his age.
  • State: his Nascent Seed has sprouted and is forming an embryonic Inner World. He is an expert, but not yet an independent power; his cultivation is a clan matter, not a private one.
  • Martial Paths: the Sword principally, the Sage secondarily, and the Self to a limited extent. The Sword follows the clan's tradition; the Sage fits his calculation, governance of routes and domains, and alliance work; the Self tempers his body, soul and will.
  • Daos: Space, the Dao the Xiao Clan is reputed for, expressed both through his sword and the clan's transportation arrays; and Yin-Yang, the balance between open appetite and precise control, read in every alliance and resource flow.

Place in the universe

  • Reputation: the Xiao Clan is renowned for its Dao of Space, and many Xiao members are legendary sword masters.
  • Immortal Plane: the clan is seen as infrastructure rather than a contending faction. No power tries to displace it, because rebuilding the array network would cost more than it would gain.
  • For the great factions: Xiao Yu stands for continuity and is their next point of contact for route access, domain leases and supply contracts. His personal favor affects array priority, so access to him has practical value.
  • Upper Worlds and Mortal Plane: the clan is the distant gatekeeper deciding which shipments and people reach the Immortal Plane. Xiao Yu's name reaches them as the future guarantor of those links.
  • Spirit Realms and Astral Expanse: the clan meets them through Saint-level scouts and array stabilizers, and can project Immortal and Eternal force through its array hubs.
  • Clan servants: Qi Ling, divine beasts, spiritual beasts and subdued demons serve as clan personnel.

Background

  • Birth: born on the Immortal Plane, in the clan's primary estate at a major array nexus.
  • Upbringing: he was raised inside active array nodes rather than in seclusion, on array diagrams, resource ledgers and Dao lectures from Immortal elders, with Eternal elders correcting him. He had unrestricted access to the clan's resources and to bound Qi Ling tutors.
  • Milestones:
  • Born at Transcendent, as Immortal Plane natives are.
  • At fifty, diagnosed and stabilized minor array nodes on his own.
  • Sovereign before one hundred.
  • Supreme between 180 and 190.
  • Defining moments:
  • He recalibrated a secondary hub array that was drifting under multi-world traffic, which stabilized transit for three worlds and pushed his Space Dao far enough to sprout his Nascent Seed.
  • He negotiated a long-term supply compact in person with a divine-beast lineage holding a contested resource domain. It secured the domain for the clan and deepened his Yin-Yang insight.
  • Why so fast: innate Dao affinity, continuous high-grade resources, and protection that removed ordinary setbacks. The breakthrough was recorded as unusually clean.

Relationships

  • Father: Late Eternal, the current patriarch of the Xiao Clan. He gives Xiao Yu full briefings on the array network and clan affairs.
  • Mother: Late Immortal, keeper of the ledgers of resource domains and factional obligations. She includes him in every major allocation, so his personal favor and the clan's interest stay aligned.
  • Immortal and Eternal elders: his instructors and overseers. Some are on permanent rotation over his cultivation and movements, and they give him real array segments and domain reviews, not ceremonial duties.
  • Guardian ancestors (past Celestial): passive watchers who step in only on clan-wide array or succession matters. They have twice sent brief spatial imprints confirming his course.
  • Protectors:
  • Two Peak Saint human array-defense specialists.
  • One Late Saint divine beast in human form, under a bloodline contract of service.
  • Several Qi Ling of Supreme-grade transportation vessels, who act as attendants and as extensions of the arrays.
  • Spiritual beasts and subdued demons, but only in larger escort formations.
  • Artifacts and Qi Ling: he owns several artifacts and treasures. Two of them are swords with powerful awakened Qi Ling, and he has established the artifact bond with both.
  • Maid: a True Dragon of ancient bloodline, serving as his personal maid.
  • Peers and rivals: heirs of great factions that depend on the Xiao arrays. He deals with them through formal exchanges and joint resource inspections. Rivals are treated as negotiation counterparts whose access depends on good terms, not as enemies.
